What areas does Tony Carlos Law serve?

Tony Carlos Law serves Yuba City, Marysville, and the surrounding areas including Sutter County, Yuba County, Colusa County, Butte County, and other Northern California communities.

What types of cases does Tony Carlos handle?

Tony Carlos handles criminal defense cases including DUI/DWI, drug crimes, theft, assault, domestic violence, felonies, and misdemeanors. He also handles immigration law matters including deportation defense, immigration court representation, asylum cases, and work permit issues.
